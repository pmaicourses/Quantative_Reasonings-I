COURSE OVERVIEW

Course Description

It introduces basic quantitative concepts and teaches students how to apply numbers, arithmetic operations, percentages, ratios, proportions, algebra, graphs, and data interpretation to solve academic and real-life problems.The course emphasizes logical thinking, accuracy, and practical application of mathematical concepts.

Topics include logical reasoning, numerical skill, fraction, percentage applications of Ratios percentage.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

Course Learning Outcomes

01

Understand fundamental concepts of Quanatative Reasoning-1.

02

Apply quantitative reasoning techniques to solve mathematical and real-world problems involving numerical data, logical reasoning, percentages, ratios, proportions, and basic algebra..

03

Apply quantitative reasoning techniques to solve numerical and mathematical problems nterpret their applications in real-life situations.

04

Develop quantitative and logical reasoning skills to analyze and solve mathematical, scientific, and real-world problems effectively.
