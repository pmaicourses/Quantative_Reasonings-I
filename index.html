COURSE OVERVIEW

Course Description

Calculus-I introduces students to the fundamental concepts of differential and integral calculus. The course develops mathematical reasoning and provides a foundation for applications in science, engineering, computing and data-driven fields.

Topics include limits, continuity, differentiation, applications of derivatives, integration and applications of integrals.

LEARNING OUTCOMES

Course Learning Outcomes

01

Understand fundamental concepts of limits and continuity.

02

Apply differentiation techniques to mathematical problems.

03

Solve integration problems and interpret their applications.

04

Develop mathematical reasoning for scientific and computational problems.
